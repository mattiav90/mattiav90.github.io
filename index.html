About

I work on asynchronous (quasi-delay-insensitive) VLSI circuit design in Professor Rajit Manohar's group at Yale. My research builds a fully asynchronous deep learning accelerator for sparse convolutional neural networks in energy-constrained settings — hardware described in ACT, verified bit-exactly against a software golden model.

Before that I worked on silicon-integrated Ti:sapphire lasers in Professor Hong Tang's lab. I hold a B.S. and M.S. from the Università degli Studi di Brescia, and an M.S. and M.Phil. from Yale University.

Current projects

SPACE Accelerator

An asynchronous, handshake-based CNN inference accelerator written in ACT. A tile-based stationary-weight PE array that skips zero weights and zero activations, runs int8 quantized convolutions, and is verified instruction-by-instruction against a Python golden model and ONNX Runtime. Current work: filling the array, cutting halo waste, and co-designing the pruning scheme around the hardware's real cost model.
